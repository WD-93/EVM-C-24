The trivial pattern _ can be eliminated: Con {field: _, rest} => Con {rest}

Adding support for Array{!ix: v} would be interesting, but that's FW.
Con {} for tag scheme Nil can be normalized to _, as can Array with all _s.
Array patterns can be normalized to a nonempty list of index-p pairs.
Unbox _ can also be normalized to _.
