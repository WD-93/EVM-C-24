What are the minimal opts I can start with?
Just SSA.
First opts: inlining, dead var elimination applied to the whole
function graph. $ret is dynamic, and may be assumed to consume all of
its arguments in general.
If a function is only called statically, its args may be changed; you
can delete the dead vars. Then the params passed at all call sites
must be changed.
If all calls to f have a given property (e.g. param x = param y), you
can use that while optimizing. Need a disjunction op for facts... is
it a lattice? param = symE is a fact.
In jumps which are calls (f,args,retf,args'...), retf,args' should be
considered a call site. There may be multiple such call frames in
a jump, cf f(g(h(x))).
A param at a jumpi call site is dead iff it's dead in both branches.
However, that doesn't mean the var is dead: a var is dead iff it has
no live uses. An op is dead iff all of its lhs vars are dead.

var is.

in the map then it's in the LHS.

*Stack****************************************************************

In later stack-scheduling phase: recall treegraph scheduling.
Tree scheduling is trivial, modulo opportunistic use of words ToS.
 That can be applied greedily; it modifies the remaining trees.
Trees can be partially executed to expose constants for sharing,
letting you recover sharing after splitting small constants to
eliminate false dependencies.

Complication: ops can consume resource vars, meaning any ops that 
borrow that var must be scheduled before them (if at all).
